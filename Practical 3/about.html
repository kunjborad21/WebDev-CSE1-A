About Student Hub

Our Mission

The Student Hub portal was created to bridge the gap between students, faculties, and the administration of CHARUSAT University. We aim to provide a seamless digital experience where students can access all their academic information in one place.

Designed specifically for the modern student, our responsive platform ensures that you stay updated whether you are on your phone, tablet, or desktop.

Key Features

  • Real-time attendance monitoring
  • Secure fee payment tracking
  • Assignment and submission schedules
  • Instant university announcements
  • Direct contact with administration
