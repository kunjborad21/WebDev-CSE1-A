Welcome to Student Hub

Student Hub is an online portal designed for students of CHARUSAT University. It helps students manage their academic life from one place. You can check your attendance, view fee status, track assignments and stay updated with announcements.
About Our College

CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) is located in Changa, Gujarat. It is a leading university known for its quality education in Engineering, IT, Pharmacy and other fields. The university provides a great learning environment with modern labs, experienced faculty and excellent placement support.
