Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the Student Hub portal.

What is Student Hub?
Student Hub is the official student portal for CHARUSAT University to manage attendance, fees, assignments, and more.
How to check attendance?
Navigate to the Attendance section from the main menu to view your detailed subject-wise attendance.
How to pay fees?
Go to the Fees section, select the semester, and use any standard payment method to securely pay your fees.
Can I update my profile details?
Yes, you can edit your profile information from the Dashboard section.
Where do I find assignments?
The Assignments page lists all your pending and completed tasks along with deadlines.
Who should I contact for technical issues?
Please visit the Contact page to reach out to the IT support team.
Is there a mobile app?
Currently, the portal is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers. A dedicated app is in development.
How do I reset my password?
Click on "Forgot Password" on the Login page to receive a password reset link on your registered email.
