Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my attendance?

Go to the Attendance page from the navigation menu. It lists your attendance percentage for each subject.

How do I reset my password?

On the Login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your email to receive a reset link.

Where can I see my fee status?

The Fees page shows your payment status for each semester.
